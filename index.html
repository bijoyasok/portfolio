Travel e-commerceWorkflow redesignHotelHub

Booking flow: from 7 steps to 4

Enterprise hotel-booking marketplace used by leading travel management companies, with 1M+ hotel properties and 1.8M+ bookings a month.

Challenge

The core booking journey was long and repetitive, so agents spent too much time on every reservation.

What I did

Led a team of 4. Used usability analysis to find redundant steps, merged them, and shipped across 15 releases, with every change confirmed in UAT before pilot and production.

Result

Steps cut from 7 to 4 and average booking time reduced by 50%.
